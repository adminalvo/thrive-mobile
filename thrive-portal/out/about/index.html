About Thrive Portal

Thrive Portal is a unified academic management environment designed to connect education center administrators, teachers, students, and parents with complete transparency and real-time efficiency.

For Students

Students can log in to view their up-to-date weekly class schedule, track attendance, check grades and scores for every lesson, submit assignments digitally, and receive direct teacher feedback.

Timetable, scoring matrix, and feedback

For Educators

Faculty members can manage their assigned groups, mark student attendance in seconds with past-date security locks, evaluate daily academic performance, and assign or review homework.

Rapid attendance marker with past-date security lock

For Parents & Guardians

Parents have 24/7 visibility into their child's educational progress, attendance records, exam results, teacher feedback, and tuition payment history — all in a single intuitive interface.

24/7 verified attendance and tuition transparency

Real-Time Synchronization

All records are synchronized directly with the center's central database, ensuring teachers, students, and parents always see accurate and verified information.
